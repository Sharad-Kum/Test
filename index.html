Three decades across Accenture, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Wipro and HCL — and in recent years working with enterprises and start-ups as advisor, consultant and full-time employee — operating across Americas, UK & Europe, ANZ and ASEAN. Three things have remained consistent: owning GTM motions for growth, translating technology into commercially viable enterprise Solutions, and building Delivery organisations at scale — while repeatedly being brought in to salvage at-risk red-flagged portfolios, solutions and business units, rebuilding stakeholder confidence and creating a credible path toward scalable growth.

The GTM work has spanned pipeline development, opportunity qualification, techno-commercial solution shaping, RFI/RFP/RFQ responses and stakeholder engagement — owning the bridge between client ambition, commercial viability and delivery credibility. Consistently 65%+ win rates. Led ELA negotiations and joint-GTM strategies with HP, IBM and CA Technologies and regional providers to often turn competitive landscapes into strategic partnerships across IT, Cloud and Digital Transformation.

Architecturally, the work has spanned 7R-based cloud migration for 6,000+ applications across hybrid and multi-cloud estates, API-first digital ecosystems integrating commerce, BPM and channel layers, and DevSecOps platforms blending service virtualisation, release automation and model-based testing into a single CI/CD lifecycle. Digital transformation extended into Health, Commerce and Marketing — from regulatory-aligned Digital Health and Life underwriting journeys to omni-channel commerce and marketing platforms — while real-time telemetry and observability frameworks closed the loop, turning KPI-driven governance and predictive defect models into faster issue resolution and more predictable delivery, with AI and ML increasingly embedded into how these systems are designed rather than bolted on afterward.

On delivery — scaled a 34 FTE pilot to 425 FTE global Delivery CoE across 7 business lines in 18 months at Accenture. Built a SaaS-enabled DevOps practice to $20M in Year 1 at Tech Mahindra. Salvaged a 43 FTE red-flagged portfolio to 180 FTE in 10 months at Capgemini. Across these engagements, delivery methodology matured from Empirical → Agile → DevOps, with AI, ML and NLP increasingly embedded into how solutions were architected and delivered.

The thought leadership thread has run parallel — co-authoring a globally published Testing Practitioners' Handbook, authoring whitepapers and process handbooks across DevOps, Quality Engineering and Delivery Governance, and representing organisations as plenary keynote speaker and jury member at national and international forums and with the analyst community.

Across all of these — the pattern across Industries, Markets and Customers has remained consistent. Process exists. Systems exist. Data exists. The need has always been connecting these layers intelligently. Technology creates possibilities. Adoption happens only when that capability is translated into what the business actually needs to hear.

The articulation of capability and outcome is where trust is won or lost.
