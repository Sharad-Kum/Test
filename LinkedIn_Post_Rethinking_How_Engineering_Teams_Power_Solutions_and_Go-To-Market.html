Rethinking How Engineering Teams Power Solutions & GTM

An observation from the intersection of technology, solutions and markets

For decades, engineering organizations have been structured around layers — frontend, backend, testing, infrastructure. Efficient for managing technology, but often misaligned with how value is delivered to customers.

When engineering aligns to solutions and customer journeys, releases are no longer technology-driven — they become market-ready increments.

Organisations that have already shifted
🇺🇸USA
Amazon · Spotify · Netflix
Amazon's Two-Pizza Teams align to services, not layers
Spotify's Squads & Tribes align to user journeys
Netflix's Full-Cycle Ownership embeds end-to-end accountability within a single team
Structure follows value, not function
